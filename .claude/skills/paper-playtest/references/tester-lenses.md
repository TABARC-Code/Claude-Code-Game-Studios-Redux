# Tester lenses

Read by `/paper-playtest` (recruiting and debrief) and `/exploit-hunt` (the optional lens pass).

These are play styles, not people. Use them to recruit a mixed table and to discount what each kind of
tester tends to miss. Most real testers blend two or three. Nothing here is a substitute for real players:
a pass through these lenses without people is SIMULATED and is never evidence.

| Lens | What they probe well | What they tend to miss |
|---|---|---|
| Optimiser | dominant lines, mispriced pieces, broken combos | accessibility, mood, first-play experience |
| Competitor | balance between equals, first-seat advantage | casual fun, teaching load |
| Story player | dissonance, flat moments, theme fit | elegance, edge-case exploits |
| Co-operator | quarterbacking, shared decision load | competitive balance |
| Social player | table talk, kingmaking, mood | deep strategy, late balance |
| Explorer | variety, discovery, unusual options | late-game balance, repeat-play depth |
| Low-pressure player | teach time, intimidation, recovery from mistakes | power curve, expert lines |
| House-ruler | where the rules are silent | clarity: they patch gaps without saying so |
| Systems analyst | structure, maths, edge cases | feel, pacing in the room |
| Teacher | second-hand teachability, reference needs | over-weights the first play |

Genre watch-points, to brief the table:

| Genre or form | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Heavy economic | downtime, analysis paralysis |
| Expansion and conquest | snowballing, decisive conflict that kills the table |
| Historical or simulation | command friction that feels arbitrary |
| Referee-led | referee burden, prep time |
| Miniatures | line-of-sight disputes, measuring arguments |
| Party | elimination downtime, scaling past six |
| Solo | setup burden, upkeep load |
| Any | symbol redundancy for colour-blind players, text size |

Returning testers learn the meta and lose patience with clarity questions. Rotate fresh testers in when the
question is whether the text can be understood cold.
