# Adventure Critique Checklist

Read by `/adventure-forge` (Phase 7 and the report). Run every row in order. For each, record
`pass`, `fail` with location (file and heading, or scene name), or `not run` with the reason.
A row left blank is reported under `Not run`, never as a pass.

## Part 1: Structural checks

| # | Area | Check | Fails as |
|---|---|---|---|

## Part 2: Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Symptom in the text | Fix to propose |
|---|---|---|
| Same encounter whichever path | Branches rejoin at an identical scene | Make paths differ in cost, information or position on arrival |
| Gatekeeper clue | One clue, one source, one roll | Add two more routes; turn the roll into a cost, not a gate |
| Exposition dump | A long speech or document carries several revelations at once | Split across scenes; let players earn each piece |
| One-enemy climax | A single foe, no phases, no choice | Add phases, a terrain change, or a choice between two goods |
| Unordered fetch list | Collect several items in any order with nothing changing | Give each item a cost or a consequence that alters the next |
| Status-quo reset | Victory returns the world to how it began | Change the equilibrium: someone gains, loses or moves |
| Attrition-only fight | A fight that only drains resources | Each encounter must reveal, escalate, cost or characterise |
| Required choice | A beat that collapses if players choose otherwise | Rewrite as pressure with at least two viable responses |

## Part 3: Symptom to cause

| Complaint | Likely cause | Look at |
|---|---|---|
| Hollow ending | The need was ignored | Phase 2 want and need |
| Shapeless middle | No turn from reacting to acting | Phase 3 beats |
| Players stuck | A chain where a web was needed | Phase 4 |
| Players drift and dawdle | Hooks without clocks | Phase 5 step 1 |
| Villain feels flat | No presence before the confrontation | Phase 6 |
| Stakes feel weightless | No personal rung | Phase 5 step 2 |

## Part 4: Output check

Before the report goes out: at most two fatal issues, each with location and fix; at least one
strength named; no invented problems; every `not run` row listed under `Not run`; campaign rows
marked `Out of scope` when campaign mode is off.
